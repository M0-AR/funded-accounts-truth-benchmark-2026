🌱 Beginner guide — read this and trade like a professional

Seven ideas. Each one saves beginners months of losses. Each one is proven by an experiment below.

  1. Your account is smaller than you think. A $100k account with a 10% limit = $10k of real risk money. An 8% profit target is really an 80% sprint on your risk money. Always compare accounts by risk money, never by headline size. (Experiment E1)
  2. Profit can kill you under trailing rules. Some accounts raise your loss floor every time you make money — a winning week leaves you less room. Beginners should choose static-loss accounts. (Experiment E2)
  3. The daily limit is the real boss. Most accounts die in a single bad day, not slowly. Set your own stop at half the firm's limit and close the laptop when it hits. Breaches drop ~41%. (Experiment E3)
  4. Staring at charts 12 hours a day is the disease. Five trades a day at 2% risk with revenge doubling dies in days; one calm trade a day at 0.5% survives for years — on the same market and signal. (Experiment E4)
  5. Passing the test ≠ getting paid. One lucky +5% day can still fail you if a single day holds 83% of your profit (consistency rule). Spread gains across days. (Experiment E7)
  6. Risk has a sweet spot — both directions fail. 0.25% per trade is too timid to ever reach the target; 2% trips the daily alarm (91% death). ~0.75–1% maximises your chance. (Experiment E6)
  7. Budget for tuition, never borrow it. At a realistic 10% pass rate, expect ~10 attempts (~$5,000 in fees) before the first pass. Borrowed money adds pressure that guarantees mistakes. (Experiment E9)
⚠️ Golden rule from the data: strategy + money-management + discipline — all three. Discipline without an edge still bleeds slowly (Experiment E8 proves it with a deliberately weak demo strategy). Paper-trade first, then risk only money you can lose.

❓ FAQ

Which account should a beginner choose?

Prefer a two-step static-loss account with the smallest size: the floor never moves, fees are lowest per risk-dollar, and the evaluation itself trains discipline (E1, E2, E5).

How much should I risk per trade?

Around 0.5–1% of headline (≈5–10% of your risk money), with a personal daily stop at half the firm's limit. Below that you can't reach targets; above it the daily limit executes you (E3, E6).

Why did my profitable account still fail?

Almost always one of three: an intraday-trailing floor rose under you (E2), a single day broke the daily limit (E3), or one hero day violated consistency (E7). Check them in that order.

Can I use my own strategy?

Yes — replace the signal in src/strategies.py, keep everything else, run make e8. The wrapper tells you which rule your strategy breaks first.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a measurement instrument. It shows base rates, mechanisms and tuition maths so you can decide with open eyes.
